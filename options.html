Select Translate

Select Translate works without a key. Chrome's on-device translator, Microsoft and Google translate your Selections out of the box. Add an OpenRouter key to use AI Models as a backup, or pick one in the Translation Card's “Translate with another entry” menu.

Providers

Your OpenRouter key stays in this browser and is sent only to openrouter.ai. Create one at openrouter.ai/keys.

Fallback Chain

Tried from top to bottom until one entry answers. An entry that reports a rate limit is skipped until its Cooldown ends.
